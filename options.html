Where your data goes
  • Browsing statistics stay in chrome.storage.local on this machine. Nothing is uploaded. View them on the dashboard, or export as JSON.
  • To-dos are stored locally too.
  • Prayer times come from the public Aladhan API using your approximate location, looked up once and then cached. Verses come from alquran.cloud and the recitation streams from everyayah.com.
  • Voice calls are the only feature that contacts the server above, and only to answer "who is online". The audio itself is peer-to-peer.
  • Leave the fields above blank and the extension never talks to a server at all.
