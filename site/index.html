Tab Tracker

Know where your hours actually go.

Tab Tracker counts the time you really spend in every app and on every site — quietly, in the background, and stored on your own machine.

Download

Desktop pill

Tracks time per application across your whole computer, not just the browser.

Download for Windows

Run the installer. The pill appears on top of your windows and starts counting.

Browser extension

Tracks sites and active time, with a timer badge on every page, to-dos and reminders.

Download for Chrome & Edge

Unzip it. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and choose the folder.

What it does

  • Counts active time only while you're really there — focused window, not idle.
  • A quiet timer badge on every page, showing today's time on that site.
  • To-dos, overuse alerts when you linger too long on a site, and prayer-time reminders.
  • Local by default. Team sync and voice calls stay optional.
